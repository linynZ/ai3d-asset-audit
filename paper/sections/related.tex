\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Image-to-3D generators.} Feed-forward and diffusion-based systems now
produce textured meshes from one image in seconds to minutes:
TripoSR~\cite{tochilkin2024triposr}, InstantMesh~\cite{xu2024instantmesh},
SF3D~\cite{boss2025sf3d}, TRELLIS~\cite{xiang2025trellis} and the Hunyuan3D
series~\cite{zhao2025hunyuan3d20,hunyuan2025hunyuan3d21}, whose commercial
service generated our assets~\cite{tencent2026hunyuan3dapi}. Several describe
their output as production- or game-ready; Hunyuan3D Studio adds retopology, UV
unwrapping and rigging for that purpose~\cite{lei2025hunyuan3dstudio}, and our
boss came from it. These claims are made and checked by the developers of each
system.

\paragraph{Evaluating generated 3D\@.} Benchmarks such as
T3Bench~\cite{he2023t3bench}, GPTEval3D~\cite{wu2024gpteval3d} and
3DGen-Bench~\cite{zhang2025threedgenbench} score rendered views for plausibility,
prompt alignment and human preference. A second line, from
MeshGPT~\cite{siddiqui2024meshgpt} to MeshAnything~\cite{chen2025meshanything,chen2024meshanythingv2},
argues that densely extracted meshes are wasteful for storage and rendering and
changes the generator's output representation toward artist-like topology. We
found no independent audit of a generator's output after it enters a real-time
engine, covering units, axes, pivots, UV efficiency, memory and animation. We
searched arXiv and the web rather than every venue, so we claim only that such
work is not prominent.

\paragraph{Mesh processing.} Our measurements use standard tools: quadric error
simplification~\cite{garland1997qem} and its extensions to appearance
attributes~\cite{garland1998colorqem,hoppe1999newqem}, texture-aware
simplification~\cite{sander2001tmpm}, Hausdorff error as computed by
Metro~\cite{cignoni1998metro}, and MeshLab~\cite{cignoni2008meshlab} through
PyMeshLab~\cite{muntoni2021pymeshlab}. Attene et al.\ survey mesh defects and
repair from an application perspective~\cite{attene2013repair}; our hygiene checks
cover a subset of the defect classes they list. Texture formats and their costs follow standard
references~\cite{akenine2018rtr4,microsoft_bc7}, and the asset format is
glTF~2.0~\cite{khronos2021gltf}.

\paragraph{Grounding animated characters.} Keeping feet on the ground during
playback is usually a per-frame problem, handled by foot-contact cleanup or inverse
kinematics~\cite{kovar2002footskate}. Engines expose bounds for skinned meshes that
are not recomputed from the current pose~\cite{unity_localbounds},
and a pose snapshot API whose scale handling depends on the
hierarchy~\cite{unity_bakemesh}; both figure in our results.
