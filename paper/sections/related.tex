\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Image-to-3D generators.} Feed-forward and diffusion systems produce
textured meshes from one image in seconds to minutes:
TripoSR~\cite{tochilkin2024triposr}, InstantMesh~\cite{xu2024instantmesh},
SF3D~\cite{boss2025sf3d}, TRELLIS~\cite{xiang2025trellis} and the Hunyuan3D
series~\cite{zhao2025hunyuan3d20,hunyuan2025hunyuan3d21}, whose commercial
service made our assets~\cite{tencent2026hunyuan3dapi}. Several properties we
measure follow from such designs. Hunyuan3D~2.1 normalises training shapes to a
unit cube and extracts surfaces as isosurfaces~\cite{hunyuan2025hunyuan3d21},
which predicts unit scale and closed meshes; its open-source texturing defaults to
4096$^2$ maps and unwraps with default xatlas settings~\cite{hunyuan21code}, and
its developers note that xatlas ``generates over-fragmented
cuts''~\cite{lei2025hunyuan3dstudio}. We cannot confirm that the commercial
service runs the same code.

\paragraph{Generated assets in production.} Evaluation of generated 3D is mostly
image-based: T3Bench~\cite{he2023t3bench}, GPTEval3D~\cite{wu2024gpteval3d} and
3DGen-Bench~\cite{zhang2025threedgenbench} score rendered views. Work on
artist-like topology~\cite{siddiqui2024meshgpt,chen2025meshanything} changes the
output representation instead. A 2026 survey of production-ready generation
observes that packing efficiency, manifoldness and watertightness are ``rarely
reported'' or ``almost never quantified''~\cite{wu2026productionready}. PartUV
measures chart counts on 114 TRELLIS meshes (xatlas: median 895, mean
1,542)~\cite{wang2025partuv}. The closest study to ours is Begemann and Hutson's
practice-led comparison, which rebuilt one game scene with Meshy and Hunyuan 3D
and reported fragmented UV maps and loss of texture integrity under
decimation~\cite{begemann2026prompted}. Practitioners describe generated UVs as
``a jumbled mess''~\cite{edwards2024spot}, and HCI studies document how solo and
indie developers use generative tools~\cite{panchanadikar2024solo}. Our study
differs in measuring every generated asset of a completed game, per asset,
against stated hypotheses, and in tracing each problem to where in the pipeline
it arose.

\paragraph{Conventions and import.} glTF fixes units (metres), up ($+Y$) and front
($+Z$), and requires a separate vertex wherever an attribute is
discontinuous~\cite{khronos2021gltf}; Khronos's importer for Blender offers to
merge such vertices only as an option~\cite{khronos_blenderio}. Khronos's
real-time asset guidelines ask for 1:1 real-world scale and an origin at the
bottom centre~\cite{khronos3dc2025guidelines}, and engine vendors note that
textures usually dominate memory~\cite{unity_mobileart}. Several items of our
checklist therefore restate existing guidance; what this case adds is which
violations occurred, where they came from and which ones went unnoticed.

\paragraph{Simplification with seams.} Quadric error
simplification~\cite{garland1997qem} was extended to appearance
attributes~\cite{garland1998colorqem,hoppe1999newqem}; Hoppe's wedge structure
stores one vertex with several attribute values at a seam~\cite{hoppe1999newqem}.
Texture-aware simplification bounds texture
stretch~\cite{sander2001tmpm}, seam-aware methods preserve the parametrisation
exactly~\cite{liu2017seamless}, and recent work simplifies
textured meshes independently of their UV layout~\cite{liu2025wild}.
meshoptimizer's documentation states that ``identical vertices'' must be welded
before simplification~\cite{meshopt}; PyMeshLab's documentation says its
per-wedge conversion ``does not merge superfluous vertices''~\cite{pymeshlabdocs};
a simplifier evaluated on Hunyuan3D output merges coincident vertices as
standard pre-processing~\cite{bhosikar2026faqem}; and an open-source game project
hit the same cracking on Meshy assets and also welded first (then re-unwrapped
and re-baked), two weeks before we wrote this~\cite{galashots2026weld}. The mechanism is known;
we report how much damage it did in a shipped asset and that the filter gave no
sign of it.

\paragraph{Grounding animated characters.} Keeping feet on the ground is a
per-frame problem, handled by foot-contact cleanup or inverse
kinematics~\cite{kovar2002footskate}. Unity offers a per-frame ``Feet'' root mode
that ``prevents [the] floating problem'', but only for Humanoid
rigs~\cite{unity_rootmotion}; our boss is a Generic rig. Unity precomputes a skinned
mesh's bounds from its animations unless asked to update them every
frame~\cite{unity_localbounds}, and its pose snapshot API has a scale option whose
effect depends on the hierarchy~\cite{unity_bakemesh}.
