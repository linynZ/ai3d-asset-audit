\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}

The audit plan---metrics, hypotheses and decision rules---was committed and
pushed to the study repository (commit \texttt{2b0bc54}) before any audit script
was written. One disclosure: while scoping the study the same day, a read-only
survey had already parsed the GLB headers and reported triangle counts, texture
sizes, the double-sided flag, the root rotation and unit-scale heights. The plan
says so and treats the hypothesis that restates them (H1) as a descriptive
confirmation, not a test. The hygiene, simplification, history and grounding
results had not been looked at. Deviations are listed in
Section~\ref{sec:deviations}.

\paragraph{B1 Conventions.} A small GLB reader (no glTF library, so the numbers
come from the same bytes the importer reads) applies each file's node chain and
records the bounding box, the largest extent, the pivot as a fraction of the box
on each axis, node transforms, material flags and extensions, and each embedded
image's format and pixel size.
\textbf{H1:} every raw asset is scale-normalised (largest extent 0.9--1.2 units),
carries a non-identity root rotation and is double-sided.

\paragraph{B2 Geometry hygiene.} Topology is measured after welding vertices with
identical positions, since a UV seam is not a hole: connected components,
boundary and non-manifold edges, non-manifold vertices, degenerate and duplicate
faces, self-intersecting faces (MeshLab~\cite{cignoni2008meshlab} via
PyMeshLab~\cite{muntoni2021pymeshlab}), and genus. UV charts are counted on the
unwelded mesh, where seams separate them; UV coverage is the union of the UV
triangles rasterised at 2048$^2$.
\textbf{H2:} a majority of the 24 raw assets fail at least one of watertight,
edge-manifold, or single component.

\paragraph{B3 Cost.} Geometry bytes as stored (vertex attributes plus indices) and
texture bytes for three GPU formats, each with a full mip chain ($\times 4/3$):
uncompressed RGBA8 (4\,B/pixel), BC7 (1\,B/pixel) and BC1 (0.5\,B/pixel).
\textbf{H3:} texture memory exceeds geometry memory at least tenfold for every
raw asset under every format.

\paragraph{B4 Simplification.} Quadric edge collapse~\cite{garland1997qem},
in its texture-aware form~\cite{garland1998colorqem,hoppe1999newqem}, as
implemented in MeshLab, with quality threshold 0.3 and boundary preservation off,
at 50, 25, 12.5 and 6.25\,\% of the original face count, up to three passes.
The plan named a with-UV and a no-UV condition. Because B2 showed that the shipped
companion had been torn (Section~\ref{sec:hygiene}), the with-UV condition was run
on two input representations of the same file: \emph{S}, the seam-split vertex
buffer that MeshLab's glTF loader returns (what our companion pipeline used), and
\emph{W}, the same mesh with exact duplicates welded and UVs kept per corner.
\emph{N} is welded with no UVs. For each run we record faces reached, open edges
after welding, components, and sampled Hausdorff distance~\cite{cignoni1998metro}
in both directions as a percentage of the bounding-box diagonal.
\textbf{H4:} with UVs preserved, a majority of assets cannot reach 6.25\,\%
within three passes, while the no-UV control can.

\paragraph{B5 History.} All non-merge commits in the game repository up to the
shipped version (484) were searched with a pre-registered case-insensitive
pattern of 25 terms (scale, pivot, ground, clip, cull, bounds, glb, decimat, \ldots).
Each of the 156 matches was labelled from its message and diff as
\texttt{ASSET} (root cause is a property of a generated asset),
\texttt{ENGINE} (an engine or tool behaviour any asset would hit),
\texttt{LEVEL} (level geometry, placement, purchased packs, procedural content)
or \texttt{OTHER}. Two AI labellers drafted the labels, each taking half; the AI
assistant reviewed every \texttt{ASSET}, \texttt{ENGINE} and low-confidence label,
added an \emph{origin} field (did the generator ship the defect, or did our own
processing introduce it?) and recorded each override with its reason; the author
signed off. For \texttt{ASSET} fixes we note which B1--B3 check, if any, would
have flagged the property before import.
\textbf{H5:} most \texttt{ASSET} fixes concern properties that counting before
import (B1/B2) would reveal.

\paragraph{E1 Grounding (exploratory).} The boss was mis-grounded five times in
26 minutes of development. In Blender~5.1 we evaluate the skinned mesh at every
frame of each animation and take the lowest vertex as ground truth, then score
the estimators the game tried: bind-pose bounds, a single frame, and the shipped
minimum over ten samples 0.16\,s apart. Errors are reported at game scale (10\,m).
