The two extractors found 83 distinct claims about the generated assets, 62 of
them independently. Judge agreement before resolution was 76\,\% (Cohen's
$\kappa = 0.64$); all 20 disagreements were resolved under a rule fixed in
advance (a verdict needs a measurement of the asset concerned; inferences from
other assets go to \texttt{UNTESTABLE}). Forty claims could not be tested
against this study's measurements and four concerned non-generated models. Of
the 39 testable claims, 31 were confirmed and eight were contradicted or only
partly supported (20.5\,\%), so H6, set at 20\,\%, is met narrowly; under the
second judge's verdicts alone the share is 31\,\% (16 of 51).

The pattern is clearer than the rate (Table~\ref{tab:claims}). The confirmed
claims are mostly numbers read off a file: triangle counts, texture sizes,
bounding boxes, the root rotation. The wrong ones are mostly explanations, and
the two wrong numbers were offered as explanations too: a face count at which
simplification had supposedly ``converged'', and a 0.04 that ruled out a cause. Five of the
eight blame the generator for something the pipeline did: the importer reversed
the front, our script's round limit stopped the simplification, and the origin
was where glTF puts it. The other three are a face count the shipped script
never produced, a pitch with the wrong sign, and the dismissal of the idle bob.
The ``0.04'' that ruled out the bob matches the bob in the FBX's own units
(0.041), while the hover it was compared with was in scene units, where the bob
is 0.22\,m; the development logs that would show which numbers were compared no
longer exist, so this is our reading, not an established fact.

Wrong explanations were not corrected; they were reused. Five of the six
contradicted claims were never revised (H7), and three of them, with both
partly-wrong ones, are still written in the final build's tool comments, a script
and a design note. The ``$-Z$'' explanation travelled: from the NPC
import tool (June 23) to the boss tool, whose comment calls it an
``NPC-pipeline finding'' (July 9), and to the companion's design note as an
expectation (July 15). Only one wrong claim, the companion's pitch, was revised,
after its effect (a fox standing on its head) appeared on screen five minutes
later. By kind, two of five testable causal claims were wrong against three of 14
property claims and two of 14 magnitudes (H8, exploratory; the counts are too
small to separate).
