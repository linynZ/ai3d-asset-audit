\section{The Assets}
\label{sec:corpus}

ChronoTraveler is a turn-based educational RPG built in Unity 6 (URP) by one
developer working with an AI coding assistant over two months in 2026. Its five
historical eras each have three non-player characters (NPCs); the Silk Road era
also has nine props (two camels, a horse, a cart, a stupa, a milestone, silk
bolts and spice sacks). All 24 were generated from a single front-view image each
with Tencent's Hunyuan 3D web service (image-to-3D, version 3.1, ``50k faces''
setting) and downloaded as GLB files. Two further assets came from the same
service by other routes: the companion fox, generated at a higher face setting
(497,850 triangles) and then simplified by us to 82,829 triangles, and the final
boss, produced by the service's studio pipeline, which adds retopology,
UV unwrapping, texturing, a 28-bone rig and four animations, delivered as four FBX
files sharing one 9,038-triangle mesh. Table~\ref{tab:corpus} summarises the
corpus; \texttt{data/manifest.json} lists every file with its SHA-256.

The 24 GLBs in the shipped game are byte-identical to the downloads. Nothing was
edited before import. Every correction was applied inside the engine: an editor
tool rescales each NPC to 1.7\,m, turns it 180\textdegree{} and converts its
material; a second tool does the same for the boss at 10\,m; a run-time component
(\texttt{GroundSnap}) moves the animated boss down onto its platform.
