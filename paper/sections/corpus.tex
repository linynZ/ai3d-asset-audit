\section{The Game and Its Generated Assets}
\label{sec:corpus}

ChronoTraveler is a turn-based educational RPG built in Unity~6 (URP) by one
developer working with an AI coding assistant in mid-2026 (repository history
June--August), as an MSc project. We call its final build (game commit \texttt{87fdd16}) the
\emph{completed} game; it was not released commercially. Its five historical eras
each have three non-player characters (NPCs); the Silk Road era also has nine
props (two camels, a horse, a cart, a stupa, a milestone, silk bolts and spice
sacks). All 24 were generated from one front-view drawing each with Tencent's
Hunyuan 3D web service (image-to-3D, version~3.1 according to the game's
pipeline notes, ``50k faces'' setting) and
downloaded as GLB. Two more meshes came from the same service: the companion fox,
generated at a higher setting (497,850 triangles) and simplified by us to 82,829
triangles before shipping, and the final boss, produced by the service's studio
pipeline~\cite{lei2025hunyuan3dstudio} with retopology, UV unwrapping, texturing,
a 28-bone rig and four animations, delivered as four FBX files that share one
9,038-triangle mesh. We audit 26 meshes, of which 25 shipped (Table~\ref{tab:corpus});
\texttt{data/manifest.json} lists every file with its SHA-256.

The 24 GLBs in the game are byte-identical to the downloads. They were imported
with UniGLTF~0.131 (the glTF importer bundled with UniVRM), whose default converts
glTF to Unity coordinates by reversing $Z$. An editor tool then rescaled each NPC
to 1.7\,m, turned it 180\textdegree{} and replaced its material with URP/Lit; a
second tool does the same for the boss at 10\,m; a run-time component
(\texttt{GroundSnap}) moves the animated boss down onto its platform.
