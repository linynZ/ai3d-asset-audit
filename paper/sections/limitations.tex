\section{Limitations}

\paragraph{Scope.} One generator and version, one importer, one game: 24 meshes
from one setting and two from other routes. Hunyuan 3D's service is closed and
changes over time. The 50k setting and 4096$^2$ textures were our defaults, not
a sweep. The texture findings depend on UniGLTF and our material tool; another
importer would store the maps differently. We report counts, not population
estimates.

\paragraph{No frame-time measurements.} Memory figures are texel counts and
stored sizes from the build, not profiler readings; we did not measure draw calls,
frame time or GPU residency per asset.

\paragraph{Not blind.} The author built the game, wrote the commits B5 labels
and wrote the plan. The labels were drafted by two AI labellers and reviewed by
the AI assistant and the author; there was no independent human rater. Commit
messages record what the developer believed at the time. Keyword search misses
commits that never used the words.

\paragraph{Grounding.} Ground truth comes from Blender's evaluation of the FBX,
not from the engine; the in-engine offset and the predicted sinking in the other
clips were not checked in play. The boss is one rig.

\paragraph{Appearance.} Visual fidelity to the input image was not scored. The
colour error in B4 uses the nearest original vertex and only the base colour map.

\paragraph{Authorship.} The game, this audit and this text were made with an AI
coding assistant. Plan, scripts, outputs, labels and the review reports that
prompted the revisions are public so that the numbers can be checked without
trusting the author or the assistant.

\section{Deviations from the Plan}
\label{sec:deviations}
\begin{itemize}
\item B4's with-UV condition was split into seam-split (S) and welded (W) inputs
  after B2 found the shipped companion torn.
\item After an internal fact-check and review of the first draft, we added: the
  facing renders (B1), the non-generated baseline (B2), the shipped-build texture
  readout (B3), S with boundary preservation, a matched no-UV control and a colour
  error (B4), all four clips and all starting phases (E1). A scene-memory
  comparison in the first draft was dropped: its premise (compressed textures) was
  wrong.
\item The first draft stated that UV charts do not overlap; the rasterised
  coverage cannot show that, and the claim was removed.
\item B5 labels were drafted by two labellers rather than one and reviewed twice.
  The second review moved three fixes out of \texttt{ASSET}, which changed H5's
  outcome (Section~\ref{sec:history}). The reviewer added an \emph{origin} field
  and the fix/feature split, neither of which was in the plan.
\item The plan's B3 metric was bytes as uploaded; we report bytes as stored in the
  file, then what the build stored.
\item E1 did not evaluate the shipped constant offset (0.30\,m), which corrects an
  engine-side reading we did not reproduce. The Wilson interval planned for H2
  was dropped: the 24 assets are not a random sample. Output file names differ
  from those listed in the plan.
\item A second internal fact-check of the revised draft led to the meshoptimizer
  comparison and to corrections of several numbers and phrasings; both review
  rounds are in the repository.
\end{itemize}
