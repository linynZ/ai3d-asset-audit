▶ 中文版（豆包/即梦，可配 cg_boss_reveal 的出图当风格参考图）：

单体游戏Boss角色设定图，全身正面直立视角，纯浅灰色背景，均匀柔和的摄影棚光照，无阴影干扰，无背景元素，3D建模参考用途，雕塑感硬表面设计。

【整体概念】一尊名为「未被书写者」的巨人石像——由五个古文明的破碎石碑残片强行拼合而成的无面巨像。它不是被建造的，而是被「钉」成这个形状的：每块残片之间接缝错位、彼此挤压，仿佛一堆废墟被一只无形的手捏成了人形。庄严、错误、亘古。

【形体结构】双足直立的宽肩驼背巨人体型，重心低沉：双腿粗壮如石柱微微分开站立，双臂过长垂至膝部、与躯干明显分离留出空隙，指节粗大如断碑；肩部宽厚隆起，头部低垂前倾——头是一面光滑的黑色石质面具，没有任何五官，只有面具中央一道垂直的骨白色裂缝，像一页纸被撕开的口子。

【五文明部件】（各部件皆为实心石材、物理咬合相连）：左肩甲是一块刻满青铜器风格饕餮纹与篆字的方形碑残片；右前臂由刻着圣书体象形文字的埃及方尖碑断段构成；左小腿是一截带凹槽的希腊多立克石柱；右肩到后背斜插着一片罗马拱券的弧形残段；胸口正中嵌着一块丝路界碑，碑上的文字已被磨平——胸腔内部透过石板缝隙可见一个嵌死在体内的球形黑色核心（完全内嵌、不悬浮），核心表面有骨白色脉络。

【材质与配色】通体为哑光的墨黑色古石材，表面布满风化剥蚀的细节与细小崩口；所有石板接缝处透出骨白色的发光脉络纹路（绘制为清晰的浅色纹样，光感克制不过曝）；部分棱角边缘残留极少量的暗金色鎏金剥落痕迹。整体黑白灰单色，仅暗金作 5% 点缀。

【姿态】静止直立微驼背，双臂自然下垂略向外撑开，五指半张，头部前倾低垂——像一尊刚被唤醒、还没学会如何存在的古老雕像。双脚踏实地面，姿态对称稳定。

【质感关键词】硬表面雕塑、石材质感、体块分明、剪影清晰、细节丰富但结构简洁、游戏角色三视图风格的正面设定图。

【避免】背景元素、地面阴影、强逆光、辉光特效、飘浮碎片、烟雾粒子、半透明、彩色光效、动态模糊、剧烈透视、水印文字。

Single game boss character reference sheet, full-body frontal standing view, plain light-grey background, even soft studio lighting, no cast shadows, no background elements, for 3D modeling reference, sculptural hard-surface design.

[Concept] A colossal stone effigy called "The Unwritten" — a faceless giant forcibly assembled from the shattered chronicle steles of five ancient civilizations. It was not built; it was PINNED into this shape: every fragment sits misaligned against its neighbors, crushed together, as if a heap of ruins had been squeezed into a humanoid form by an invisible hand. Solemn, wrong, and ancient beyond memory.

[Body structure] Bipedal, wide-shouldered, hunched giant with a low center of gravity: thick pillar-like legs planted slightly apart; overlong arms hanging to knee height, clearly separated from the torso with visible gaps; massive knuckles like broken stele stubs. Broad heaped shoulders; the head hangs low and forward — a smooth black stone MASK with no facial features at all, only a single vertical bone-white crack down its center, like a page torn open.

[Five-civilization parts] (every part solid stone, physically interlocked and connected): the left pauldron is a square stele fragment carved with bronze-ware taotie motifs and seal script; the right forearm is a broken segment of an Egyptian obelisk covered in hieroglyphs; the left shin is a fluted Greek Doric column drum; a curved fragment of a Roman arch juts diagonally across the right shoulder and back; embedded in the center of the chest is a Silk Road boundary stele whose inscription has been worn blank — and through the gaps between chest plates, a spherical black core is visibly WEDGED inside the ribcage (fully embedded, not floating), its surface traced with bone-white veins.

[Material & palette] Entire body of matte ink-black ancient stone, weathered and chipped with fine erosion detail; bone-white glowing vein patterns seep through every seam between slabs (painted as crisp pale markings, restrained glow, never blown out); a few edges retain tiny flakes of dark antique gilding. Overall monochrome black-white-grey, dark gold as a mere 5% accent.

[Pose] Standing still, slightly hunched, arms hanging naturally and braced a little outward, fingers half-open, head bowed forward — an ancient statue newly awakened, not yet sure how to exist. Both feet firmly planted, stable symmetric stance.

[Craft keywords] hard-surface sculpture, stone material rendering, bold readable volumes, clean silhouette, rich detail on simple structure, game character reference-sheet style frontal view.
